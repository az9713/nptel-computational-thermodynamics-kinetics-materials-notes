On this page

Study guide

Begin with phase identity, amount bases and conservation. Build the thermodynamic potentials and constrained derivatives before using equilibrium engines or kinetic models. The later chapters repeatedly distinguish a positive mobility from a thermodynamic curvature, a stable phase from a kinetic pathway and an experimentally assessed model from a synthetic consistency check.

How to use this edition

Read each derivation with its assumptions, attempt the problems before expanding solutions and then execute the exact downloaded program. Follow topic timestamps when comparing with recordings. Treat source-specific ambiguities and stated access limits as part of the evidence. Scientific review and numerical checks are different forms of verification; neither alone certifies a real material prediction.
