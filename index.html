Computational Thermodynamics and Kinetics of Materials

An independent study edition connecting microstructure and phase diagrams to thermodynamic models, reproducible equilibrium calculations, diffusion, precipitation and material design.

The full playlist contains sixty substantive lessons and a separate introduction. This edition has 24 authored chapters; 24 currently carry two accepted independent scientific reviews at their recorded raw body digests. Public validation records describe the release checks actually completed.

Read entirely offline: mathematics, theme and text controls, navigation, complete expandable solutions and downloadable numerical examples are bundled locally. Code checks test stipulated mathematical models; they do not establish experimental validity.
