These are the primary research, official documentation and university teaching links used in the chapters. Each local citation states the supported claim and, where appropriate, the actual pages or abstract inspected. A listed paper is not a claim that its entire text, supplementary material, cited experiments or numerical implementation was audited.

Source access limits, unavailable documents and original synthetic constructions are disclosed in the exposition. Recording links are collected separately in the source map. No downloaded third-party paper or raw lecture transcript is redistributed.
